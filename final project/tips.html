Common Questions

Can I drink tap water?

Yes! Finnish tap water is some of the cleanest and tastiest in the world.

Do I need to tip?

Tipping is not expected. Rounding up the bill for great service is fine!

What if I get lost?

Finns can be quiet, but they are very helpful. Just ask anyone in English; almost everyone is fluent!
